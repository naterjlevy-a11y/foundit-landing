Cookie Policy

Last updated: May 2026

This page explains how our website may use cookies and similar technologies. The Found It! macOS app itself does not use browser cookies.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser. They can keep you signed in, remember preferences, or help us understand aggregate traffic patterns.

2. Essential / functional

If you sign in to manage your account or subscription on our website, our authentication provider (Supabase) may set a first-party session cookie to keep you logged in. These are necessary for that functionality to work and cannot be opted out of while using the feature.

3. Analytics

We currently use no third-party analytics on this site. If we add analytics in the future, we will list the provider here and use a privacy-respecting tool that does not fingerprint individual users.

4. Marketing

We do not use advertising trackers or retargeting pixels on this site. If that changes, we will update this policy and provide opt-out information as required by applicable law.

5. Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies via your browser settings. Blocking session cookies will prevent account sign-in on the website but does not affect the macOS app.
