I kept losing files on my Mac. Not because I deleted them — because I couldn't remember what I named them or where I put them. Spotlight only searches file names. So does Google Drive. So does everything.

Found It! is different. It reads what's inside your files, builds a local index, and lets you search by describing what you remember — the topic, the class, the project, roughly when you made it. No exact filename needed.

I built this entirely on my own while studying full-time at McGill. Every line of Swift, every design decision, every Edge Function — one person.

Mission

Make your files actually findable — without giving up privacy.

Private by design

Your files stay on your Mac. Only short text excerpts travel to AI — never stored server-side.

Search by intent

Describe what you remember and Found It! figures out what you mean. No syntax, no operators.

Indie & sustainable

Funded by people who use it. No VC pressure, no ads, no selling your data.
