About Found It!

Founder-led · privacy-first · built for real Finder chaos.

NL
Nate Levy Founder, Found It!
McGill University · GitHub · LinkedIn

I'm a Mechanical Engineering student at McGill University in Montreal, Quebec. I built Found It! because I kept losing files on my Mac — and I knew I wasn't alone.

Spotlight searches file names. Google Drive searches file names. Everything searches file names. But nobody actually names their files well.

Found It! is different. It reads what's INSIDE your files and understands what you're looking for, even when you can't remember what the file was called.

I built this entirely on my own while studying full-time. Every line of code, every design decision, every feature — built by one person who just wanted a better way to find files.

ABOUT THE FOUNDER

Nate is a Mechanical Engineering student at McGill University in Montreal.

He built Found It! after spending too much time searching for files he knew existed on his Mac. Spotlight searches file names. Found It! searches what's actually inside your files.

The goal is simple: find anything in under a second — regardless of what you named it or where you put it.
