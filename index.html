FAQ

Straight answers.

Does Found It! upload my files?
No. The product is built around local-first indexing — your file contents are not uploaded to our servers for basic search. Optional cloud features, if added later, will be clearly labeled and opt-in.
What file types does it support?
Found It! is designed for the messy reality of a Mac: PDFs, Office documents, notes, markdown, images with text, code, and more. Exact coverage evolves with each release.
Is this better than Spotlight?
Spotlight is filename-first for many workflows. Found It! focuses on meaning and contents — closer to how you actually remember files.
Does it work with Google Drive or iCloud?
Core focus is local disk today. Cloud providers are on the roadmap as optional integrations — not implied in the shipping beta unless stated in-app.
What happens after the free-search limit?
During beta, limits may change. When Pro exists, upgrades will unlock higher limits — communicated clearly in the app.
Is Found It! available for Windows?
No. macOS only for now.
How do I delete my index?
You will be able to reset or delete the local index from Settings inside the app. Exact controls ship with the beta builds.
