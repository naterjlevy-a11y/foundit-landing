Questions.

Does Found It replace Spotlight?
No. Spotlight is useful when you know what something is called. Found It is for when you remember what the file was about, not its exact name.
What folders does it index?
Found It currently indexes Documents, Downloads, and Desktop. Support for additional folders is planned for a future release.
Is it a dock app?
No. Found It is menu bar only, so it stays out of your dock and out of your way until you need it.
How much does Pro cost?
Pro is $8/month and includes unlimited searches and Deep Search. You can cancel at any time through the billing portal.
Does search happen online?
Search runs against a local index on your Mac. Found It may use a lightweight query-expansion call (through a secure Edge Function) to better understand search intent. This call costs under $0.001 and the API key is never exposed in the app.
Is there a free version?
Yes. The free tier includes 3 searches per day with full local indexing. No credit card required to download.
