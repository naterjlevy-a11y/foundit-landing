§ 002 · How it works

It indexes first, then searches fast.

Found It builds a local index of your files in the background. When you search, everything is already there — no scanning, no waiting.

01

Found It indexes your files

Scans Documents, Downloads, and Desktop — building a local SQLite index with AI-generated summaries, embeddings, and smart metadata.

local folders AI summaries SQLite
02

Smart keyword extraction

Your query is lemmatized (e.g. "running" → "run"), expanded with synonyms, and used to fetch candidates from the local index via fast LIKE queries.

NLTagger lemmatization local only
03

Scored, ranked, and ready

Each candidate file is scored against your query across 10+ signals — filename, content, metadata, embeddings, recency, and date — then ranked and shown instantly.

exact / lemma / fuzzy embeddings ranked results

Standard search is fully local and near-instant. Pro Deep Search adds an optional AI pass for vague queries. Free indexes up to 500 files; Pro unlocks everything.

Questions.

Is there a free version?
Yes. Free includes unlimited searches across your first 500 indexed files. No credit card required.
How fast is the search?
Standard search is fully local — no network call. Results appear in milliseconds. Deep Search (Pro) adds an AI pass that takes 1–3 seconds but finds much more for vague queries.
What does Pro unlock?
Pro unlocks unlimited file indexing, Deep Search (AI query expansion + reranking), image conversion, drag-and-drop import and use, and priority re-indexing.
What folders does Found It index?
Documents, Downloads, and Desktop. Support for custom folders is on the roadmap.
Does Found It replace Spotlight?
No. Spotlight is great when you know the filename. Found It is for when you remember what the file was about — the topic, the class, the project — not its exact name.

I care about software that feels fast, stays out of your way, and respects your data. Found It runs entirely on your Mac — no cloud, no uploads, just results.
