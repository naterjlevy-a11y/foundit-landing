FAQ

Straight answers.

Does Found It! upload my files?
No. Core search is local-first — contents stay on your Mac unless you opt into a future network feature we clearly describe.
What file types does it support?
PDFs, Office files, notes, markdown, images with text, code, and more.
Is this better than Spotlight?
Spotlight is often filename-first. Found It! is built around meaning and file contents.
Does it work with Google Drive or iCloud?
Today the focus is local disk. Cloud providers are roadmap items.
What happens after the free limit?
Wait for the daily reset or upgrade to Pro for unlimited searches.
Windows?
macOS only.
How do I reset my index?
From Settings inside the app.
