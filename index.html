FAQ

Straight answers.

Does Found It! upload my files?
No. Core search is built around local-first indexing — file contents are not uploaded to our servers for basic search. Optional features will be clearly labeled.
What file types does it support?
PDFs, Office documents, notes, markdown, images with text, code, and more — the messy reality of a Mac disk.
Is this better than Spotlight?
Spotlight is filename-first for many workflows. Found It! focuses on meaning and contents — closer to how you remember files.
Does it work with Google Drive or iCloud?
Today the focus is local disk. Cloud providers are on the roadmap as optional integrations when privacy constraints are met.
What happens after the free-search limit?
You hit the daily cap until it resets, or upgrade to Pro for unlimited searches.
Is Found It! available for Windows?
No. macOS only.
How do I delete my index?
Reset or delete the local index from Settings inside the app.
