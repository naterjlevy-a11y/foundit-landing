FAQ

Straight answers.

Does Found It! upload my files?
No. Core search is local-first — contents stay on your Mac unless you opt into a future network feature we clearly describe.
What file types does it support?
PDFs, Office files, notes, markdown, images with text, code, and more. The indexer reads text content, metadata, and runs OCR on images.
Is this better than Spotlight?
Spotlight is filename-first. Found It! is built around meaning and file contents — it understands "math homework from last week" and returns ranked results with match reasons.
Does it work with Google Drive or iCloud?
Today the focus is local disk. Cloud providers are roadmap items.
What happens after the free limit?
You can wait for the daily reset or upgrade to Pro for unlimited searches. Free users still get full indexing and Browse/Archive features.
Windows?
macOS only. Found It! integrates tightly with macOS file APIs and the menu bar.
How do I reset my index?
From the Settings tab inside the app.
