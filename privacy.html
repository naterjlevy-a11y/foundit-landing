1. Overview

Found It! is a local-first macOS app. Your documents, their indexes, and your search queries remain on your Mac. We do not operate an "upload everything to the cloud" model.

The only data that travels off-device is a short text excerpt (up to 800 characters) sent to our AI summarisation Edge Function during indexing — and that excerpt is not stored on our servers after the response is returned.

2. What we may collect

Account: If you sign in (e.g. email / magic link), we store the email you provide in Supabase Auth for sign-in, subscription verification, and essential service messages.

Billing: Payments are processed by Stripe. We receive subscription status and basic billing metadata — never your full card number or CVV.

Crash diagnostics (optional / coarse): If you opt in, we may collect app version and crash stack traces to fix bugs. This never includes file contents or search queries.

3. What we deliberately avoid

We do not sell your personal information. We do not train generalized AI models on your private documents. We do not log the search queries you run inside the app.

4. AI summarisation

During indexing, short text excerpts (up to 800 characters) are sent to our Supabase Edge Function which calls MiniMax's API to generate keywords and a summary. The excerpt is not stored server-side after the response. The resulting keywords and summary are written to a local SQLite database on your Mac.

Images sent for vision analysis follow the same pattern: a compressed excerpt travels to the Edge Function and is not retained.

5. Local data storage

Your search index is a SQLite database stored in ~/Library/Application Support/FoundIt/index.db. You can delete it at any time. Found It! will rebuild the index from your files on next launch.

6. Retention & deletion

You can request account deletion by emailing us. We will remove your Supabase Auth record and any associated subscription data within 30 days. Operational backups may persist for up to 90 days consistent with good security practice.

7. Third-party services

We use Supabase for authentication and Edge Functions (EU/US region), Stripe for billing, and MiniMax for AI summarisation. Each operates under their own privacy policy.

8. International users

If you access the service from outside the United States or Canada, note that providers we use may process data in other regions. We work with vendors that maintain appropriate data protection safeguards.

9. Children

Found It! is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the product evolves. Significant changes will be communicated via the app or the email on your account.
