Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026 · Placeholder policy — replace with final legal review.

This policy describes how Found It! (“we”, “us”) handles information when you use our macOS application and website. We built the product around local-first indexing: your documents stay on your device unless you explicitly enable a feature that requires the network.

1. Overview

Found It! is designed so that search indexes and file contents remain on your Mac. We do not operate a “upload everything to the cloud” model for your documents.

2. What we may collect

Account: If you create an account (e.g. email), we store what you provide for sign-in, receipts, and essential service messages.

Billing: Payments are processed by Stripe. We receive limited billing metadata (e.g. subscription status), not your full card number.

Product telemetry (optional / minimal): We may collect coarse usage signals (e.g. app version, crash diagnostics) to fix bugs. Nothing here is intended to include the contents of your files.

3. What we deliberately avoid

We do not sell your personal information. We do not train generalized AI models on your private documents as part of the core local search workflow described on our marketing site.

4. Local indexing

Indexing, embeddings, and search over your files are performed locally on your machine unless a future feature explicitly states otherwise and you opt in.

5. Retention & deletion

You can request account deletion where applicable. Operational backups may persist for a limited period consistent with good security practice.

6. International users

If you access the service from outside Canada or the United States, note that providers we use may process data in other regions. We aim to work with vendors that offer appropriate safeguards.

7. Children

Found It! is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

8. Contact

Privacy questions: privacy@founditapp.com
