Refund Policy

Last updated: May 2026

We want a paid Pro plan, when it ships, to feel fair: if it is not a fit, you can reach out within the window below.

1. Pro subscriptions

If you upgrade to Pro and it is not a fit, email hello@founditapp.com within 30 days of the charge. Include the email used at checkout and approximate purchase date.

2. How refunds are sent

Approved refunds return to the original payment method when the processor allows it. Timing is usually 5–10 business days but can vary by bank.

3. Partial periods

We may issue full or partial refunds depending on usage, subscription term, and standard fraud-prevention checks.

4. Chargebacks

Reach out to us first; chargebacks slow everyone down. If there is a billing error we will correct it.
